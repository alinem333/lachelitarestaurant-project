About Us

La Chelita started back in the 70s, when it was first called La Tapatia. A family business, it was run by Graciela “Chelita” Ramirez, whose vision and tradition still inspires us to this day. We honor her legacy by putting our community first and keeping our Jalisco styled Mexican food touch.
